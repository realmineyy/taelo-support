PRIVACY

Your fitness data belongs to you.

Taelo is designed to work without an account, advertisements, tracking SDKs, or a proprietary cloud service.

Effective September 2, 2026

Overview

Taelo is operated by its independent developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Workout, outdoor-run, nutrition, hydration, supplement, fasting, routine, body-measurement, and progress-photo entries are stored locally in the app's private container on the user's device.

Data we collect

We do not collect Taelo workout, route, food, hydration, supplement, fasting, nutrition, body-measurement, or progress-photo data. In this policy, “collect” means transmitting data off the device so that we or another party can access it beyond what is necessary to complete an immediate user request.

Taelo does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not create a Taelo account or send fitness journals to our servers.

Local data and exports

Entries, custom foods, custom exercises, routines, preferences, and selected progress-photo copies remain in Taelo's private local storage. Progress photos use iOS file protection and are not uploaded by Taelo. Deleting the app generally deletes this local app data.

The workout CSV export does not include progress photos. Content shared through Apple's system share sheet is handled by the destination the user selects and is subject to that destination's privacy practices.

Location during outdoor runs

When a user intentionally starts an outdoor run and grants permission, Taelo uses device location to calculate and draw the activity's route, distance, pace, splits, and elevation. Background location allows that active recording to continue while the screen is locked or Taelo is not in the foreground. Route information remains with the local run record and is not sent to our servers.

Apple Health

If the user chooses Connect Apple Health and grants permission, Taelo can read optional fitness signals — heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, step count, active energy, and sleep analysis — to show training and recovery context inside the app. Taelo can write weight and body-fat entries, walking and running distance, active energy, and completed strength workouts or outdoor runs. Health data is read and written on the device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Health access is optional, granted per data type, and declining it does not prevent local logging.

We do not use Health data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data mining; sell it; or disclose it to data brokers. Deleting Taelo does not automatically remove samples previously written to Apple Health. Users manage those samples and permissions in Apple's Health and Settings apps.

Food barcode lookup

When a user scans a food barcode that is not already saved on the device, Taelo sends only that barcode, the app version, and ordinary network request information such as the IP address to Open Food Facts, to request community-provided product and nutrition data. A successful result is stored locally so the same product works offline without another lookup. Taelo does not send Open Food Facts a user identity, workouts, routes, Health data, nutrition diary, supplements, fasts, progress photos, or body measurements. Open Food Facts’ own privacy terms apply to that request. Aside from subscription verification described below, this is the only feature that sends anything off the device, and no fitness content is included in it.

Subscriptions and Apple services

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may provide developers with transaction, proceeds, territory, and subscription-status reports under Apple's terms and privacy policy. Taelo uses StoreKit together with RevenueCat to verify subscription access and provide aggregate subscription reporting. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous subscription identifier, product and transaction metadata, entitlement status, and limited app and device metadata needed to operate that service. It does not receive workouts, routes, Health data, nutrition entries, supplements, fasts, progress photos, or body measurements. We do not receive payment-card details.

Children

Taelo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and limitations

Keeping data on-device reduces exposure to a proprietary server, but no device or storage method is perfectly secure. Users are responsible for device access controls and any copies they export.

Changes

We may update this policy as Taelo changes. We will revise the effective date and publish the current version at this URL. If a future release introduces a server, analytics, advertising, or other data transmission, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before that release.
