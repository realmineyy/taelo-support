PRIVACY

Your fitness data belongs to you.

Taelo is designed to work without an account, advertisements, tracking SDKs, or a proprietary cloud service.

Updated September 11, 2026

Overview

Taelo is operated by its independent developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Workout, outdoor-run, nutrition, supplement, hydration, routine, body-measurement, and progress-photo entries are stored locally in the app's private container on the user's device.

Data we collect

We do not collect Taelo workout, route, food, hydration, nutrition, body-measurement, or progress-photo data. In this policy, “collect” means transmitting data off the device so that we or another party can access it beyond what is necessary to complete an immediate user request.

Taelo does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not create a Taelo account or send fitness journals to our servers.

Local data and exports

Entries, custom foods, custom exercises, routines, preferences, and selected progress-photo copies remain in Taelo's private local storage. Progress photos use iOS file protection and are not uploaded by Taelo. Deleting the app generally deletes this local app data.

Taelo can create user-requested CSV and PDF exports. These exports do not include progress-photo image files. Content shared through Apple's system share sheet is handled by the destination the user selects and is subject to that destination's privacy practices.

Location during outdoor runs

When a user intentionally starts an outdoor run and grants permission, Taelo uses device location to calculate and draw the activity's route, distance, pace, splits, and elevation. Background location allows that active recording to continue while the screen is locked or Taelo is not in the foreground. Route information remains with the local run record and is not sent to our servers.

Nearby trails and downloaded routes

When a user selects Find nearby trails, Taelo requests a one-time location and sends a rounded search area, an app identifier, and ordinary network information such as the request IP address to the U.S. Geological Survey's National Map service. The query does not include the user's precise coordinate, identity, recorded routes, or fitness journal. This search does not start an outdoor run or background tracking.

Discovery requires an internet connection and currently covers U.S. mapped trail segments. Routes the user chooses to download are stored locally as route lines and details. Taelo does not download offline basemap tiles through this feature.

Recipe catalogue

The bundled recipe catalogue, its search, and saved recipe preferences work on the device. Taelo does not send recipe searches to a server. Opening a recipe's source link uses the user's browser and is subject to that website's privacy practices.

Apple Health

If the user chooses to connect Apple Health and grants permission, Taelo can read optional heart-rate, step, active-energy, and sleep signals for a private dashboard. Taelo can write new weight and body-fat entries and completed strength workouts or outdoor runs. Health access is optional, and declining it does not prevent local logging.

We do not use Health data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data mining; sell it; send it to RevenueCat or Open Food Facts; or disclose it to data brokers. Deleting Taelo does not automatically remove samples previously written to Apple Health. Users manage those samples and permissions in Apple's Health and Settings apps.

Open Food Facts barcode lookup

When a scanned barcode is not already saved on the device, Taelo sends that barcode, the app version in an identifiable user-agent, and ordinary network information such as the request IP address to Open Food Facts to retrieve community-provided product and nutrition data. A successful result is cached locally. Taelo does not send Open Food Facts the user's identity, journal, routes, Health data, supplements, body measurements, or progress photos. Open Food Facts' own terms and privacy practices apply to the lookup.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may provide developers with transaction, proceeds, territory, and subscription-status reports under Apple's terms and privacy policy. Taelo uses StoreKit to determine whether Premium access is active and may use RevenueCat to verify entitlement access and provide aggregate subscription reporting. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous subscription support identifier, product and transaction metadata, entitlement status, and limited app or device metadata needed to operate that service. Taelo does not send RevenueCat fitness, nutrition, Health, location, supplement, body, or progress-photo data. We do not receive payment-card details.

Children

Taelo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and limitations

Keeping data on-device reduces exposure to a proprietary server, but no device or storage method is perfectly secure. Users are responsible for device access controls and any copies they export.

How Taelo is built, and AI

Taelo is built by an independent developer using ordinary software tools, including AI-assisted coding and writing tools, with human review before release. Software built this way can still contain errors, and no development method guarantees correct or complete output. Taelo is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis as described in the Terms of Use.

Taelo does not include an AI assistant, chatbot, or machine-learning service that processes your information. Your workouts, routes, nutrition entries, Health data, body measurements, photos, and other content are not sent to any AI or machine-learning provider, are not used to train any model, and are not used for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you. Barcode scanning uses Apple's on-device frameworks, and the training calculations run on your device from the entries you log.

Some in-app text, exercise descriptions, and recipe formatting were drafted or edited with AI assistance and reviewed by a person. This material is general information only, may contain mistakes, and is not medical, nutritional, or professional advice. Please verify anything you rely on, and report problems through the support tracker.

Your choices and rights

Because Taelo keeps your entries on your device, you control them directly: edit or delete any entry in the app, export CSV or PDF copies, revoke Health or location permission in iOS Settings, and delete the app to remove its local data. We cannot access, retrieve, correct, or delete data that never leaves your device, and we cannot restore it for you if the device is lost.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, our limited processing rests on your consent for optional device permissions and on our legitimate interest in operating and supporting the app; you may withdraw consent at any time in iOS Settings. You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability, exercisable in the app for on-device data, and you may complain to your local supervisory authority.

Where U.S. state privacy laws apply, including California's CCPA and CPRA: we do not sell personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and do not process it for targeted advertising or profiling. We do not knowingly process the personal information of children under 13. Exercising a privacy right will never result in discriminatory treatment.

Retention and international use

Entries stay on your device until you delete them or delete the app. Barcode results cached on the device remain until the app is deleted. We do not operate a server that stores your fitness data, so we hold no copy to retain, transfer, or disclose. Requests made to Open Food Facts and the U.S. Geological Survey are processed by those organisations, which may be located outside your country and are governed by their own policies.

Taelo is offered worldwide through the App Store. Payments, tax, and refunds are handled by Apple in your territory. Where local law gives you rights beyond this policy, those rights apply.

Changes

We may update this policy as Taelo changes. We will revise the effective date and publish the current version at this URL. If a future release introduces a server, analytics, advertising, or other data transmission, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before that release.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be submitted through the Taelo support tracker. Do not include private health, nutrition, location, or progress-photo information in a public issue. We aim to respond to privacy requests within 30 days.
