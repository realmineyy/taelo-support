QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

Where is my data?

Taelo stores entries and progress-photo copies locally on your iPhone. Version 1.0 has no Taelo account or proprietary cloud backup. Use Settings to export workout, run, nutrition, hydration, body, routine, and supplement records before deleting the app or changing devices. Exports do not contain progress-photo files, so preserve their originals separately.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Today → Settings → Explore Taelo Pro, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

How do I cancel?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Taelo, and follow Apple's controls. Deleting Taelo does not cancel Pro.

Why did Apple Health not update?

Review Health permissions for Taelo in iPhone Settings. Taelo writes only new measurements and completed activities after permission is granted. Read access for the private daily dashboard is separate, and denied permissions never prevent local logging.

Why is my route missing?

Allow location access when Taelo asks, begin the run outdoors with a clear GPS signal, and keep Location Services enabled until the activity is finished. Taelo records location only for an outdoor run you start.

Why was a barcode not found?

Taelo checks its offline catalog first and then requests the barcode from Open Food Facts when online. Community product records may be missing or incomplete, so compare every result with the package label or add the food manually.

How do I delete my data?

Delete entries in Taelo or uninstall the app to remove its local container. Manage copies in Apple Health, device backups, exports and shared files separately. Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase records after the app is deleted. For subscription-record or support-data requests, email aidenjsmith2@icloud.com; see the Privacy Policy for retention, deletion and applicable rights. Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel Pro.
