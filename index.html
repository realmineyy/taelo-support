QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

Where is my data?

Taelo stores entries and progress-photo copies locally on your iPhone. Version 1.0 has no Taelo account or proprietary cloud backup. Export your workout CSV before deleting the app or changing devices. The CSV does not contain progress photos, so preserve their originals separately.

How do I restore Pro?

Open the Profile tab, choose Taelo Pro, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

How do I cancel?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Taelo, and follow Apple's controls. Deleting Taelo does not cancel Pro.

Why did Apple Health not update?

Review Health permissions for Taelo in iPhone Settings — access is granted per data type, so one category can be off while others work. Once granted, Taelo writes weight and body-fat entries, distance, active energy, and completed workouts and runs, and reads heart rate, steps, active energy, and sleep for training context.

Why is my route missing?

Allow location access when Taelo asks, begin the run outdoors with a clear GPS signal, and keep Location Services enabled until the activity is finished. Taelo records location only for an outdoor run you start.

How do I delete my data?

Because Taelo has no account or server, there is no server-side profile to delete. Deleting the app removes its local container. Samples written to Apple Health must be managed separately in the Health app.
